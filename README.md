# My Shelf

A cute, pink app for my skincare and makeup: AM, PM, makeup and extras routines, a stash of backups with use-by dates, and (coming soon) prices across Australian stores.

- Everything I add is saved on my phone only. Nothing is stored in this project.
- Use **Back up my shelf** on the Stash page now and then, and keep the file in Google Drive.
- To install: open the site in Chrome on my phone, tap the three-dot menu, then **Add to home screen**.
